# Homie - Startup One-Pager

> **Read time**: 5 minutes | **Last updated**: January 24, 2026

---

## 1. What is this startup?

**Homie is a mobile app that helps people find rental homes without brokers.**

Think of it as **"Tinder meets Airbnb for long-term rentals"** - tenants swipe through properties, landlords swipe through tenants, and when both like each other, they match and can chat to finalize the deal.

We focus on the Indian rental market, starting with Bengaluru.

---

## 2. What does this startup do?

Homie handles the **entire rental journey** from finding a home to paying monthly rent:

| Stage | What We Do |
|-------|------------|
| **Discovery** | AI matches tenants with compatible properties (not random listings) |
| **Communication** | In-app chat between tenant and landlord (no phone number exchange) |
| **Viewing** | Schedule property visits through the app |
| **Agreement** | Digital rental agreement with e-signature |
| **Payments** | Security deposit protection + automated monthly rent |

**In simple terms**: We replace brokers, paperwork, and cash transactions with a single app.

---

## 3. Who is the customer?

We have **two customers** (it's a marketplace):

### Customer 1: Tenants (People looking for homes)
- **Who**: Young professionals (22-35 years), relocating for jobs
- **Where**: Tier-1 cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai)
- **Income**: ₹5-15 lakh/year
- **Pain point**: Hate paying ₹30-50K to brokers, can't trust random landlords

### Customer 2: Landlords (Property owners)
- **Who**: Individuals owning 1-3 rental properties
- **Where**: Same cities
- **Pain point**: Tired of unqualified inquiries, no-shows, and rent collection hassles

**Primary paying customer**: Both, but landlords pay more (they have assets at stake).

---

## 4. What problem are we solving?

### For Tenants:
| Problem | How bad is it? |
|---------|----------------|
| Broker fees | ₹30,000-50,000 per rental (1 month rent) |
| Time waste | Visit 15-20 properties over 3-4 weekends |
| Trust issues | Give ₹50K-2L deposit to stranger |
| No protection | Landlord can deduct unfairly, no recourse |

### For Landlords:
| Problem | How bad is it? |
|---------|----------------|
| Unqualified leads | 50 inquiries, only 5 serious |
| No-shows | Schedule 10 viewings, 3 actually come |
| Rent delays | Chase tenants every month |
| Bad tenants | No way to verify before handing keys |

### The Core Problem We Solve:
> **"Renting in India is a ₹4 lakh crore market running on trust, cash, and chaos. We bring structure, protection, and convenience."**

## 5. How do we acquire customers?

### Tenant Acquisition:

| Channel | How it works | Cost |
|---------|--------------|------|
| **Referrals** | "Invite friend, both get ₹500 credit" | ₹500/user |
| **Google Search** | "Flats for rent in Koramangala" → Homie | ₹50-100/click |
| **Instagram/Facebook** | Video ads showing app demo | ₹30-50/click |
| **Content** | Blog: "How to rent in Bengaluru without broker" | Free |
| **Partnerships** | Corporate HR teams (relocation assistance) | Revenue share |

**Target CAC (Cost to Acquire Customer)**: ₹500-1,000 per tenant

### Landlord Acquisition:

| Channel | How it works | Cost |
|---------|--------------|------|
| **Door-to-door** | Sales team visits apartment complexes | ₹1,000/landlord |
| **Existing landlords** | "List another property, get 1 month free" | ₹0 |
| **Property managers** | Partner with those managing 10+ properties | Revenue share |
| **Facebook Groups** | "Landlords of Bengaluru" communities | Free |

**Target CAC**: ₹1,000-2,000 per landlord

### Chicken-and-Egg Solution:
We start **supply-first** (landlords) in one neighborhood. Once we have 50 properties in Koramangala, we acquire tenants searching for that area. Concentrated supply → concentrated demand.

## 6. How do we earn money? (Revenue Model)

We make money at **5 points** in the rental journey:

### Revenue Stream 1: Viewing Deposit
| What | Tenant pays ₹1,000 to schedule viewing (refundable) |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Why | Filters serious tenants, prevents no-shows |
| We earn | ~5% forfeit rate = ₹50 per viewing average |

### Revenue Stream 2: Token Payment
| What | Tenant pays ₹5,000 to "lock" property after viewing |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Why | Creates commitment, prevents bypass |
| We earn | 1.5% escrow fee = ₹75 |

### Revenue Stream 3: Security Deposit Escrow
| What | We hold ₹50,000-2,00,000 deposit safely |
|------|----------------------------------------|
| Why | Protection for tenant (fair refund), protection for landlord (guaranteed) |
| We earn | 1.5% fee + 6% interest on float |

**Example**: ₹75,000 deposit × 1.5% = ₹1,125 fee + ₹4,500 annual interest = **₹5,625 per deal**

### Revenue Stream 4: Rent Automation
| What | Auto-debit rent on 1st of every month |
|------|--------------------------------------|
| Why | Convenience for both parties |
| We earn | 1% of rent (shared as cashback with tenant) |

**Example**: ₹25,000 rent × 1% × 11 months = **₹2,750 per year**

### Revenue Stream 5: Premium Subscriptions
| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Tenant Free | ₹0 | 10 swipes/week |
| Tenant Plus | ₹49/month | Unlimited swipes, priority |
| Landlord Pro | ₹299/month | Featured listing, tenant insights |

### Total Revenue Per Transaction:
```
Viewing deposit profit:     ₹50
Token payment fee:          ₹75
Escrow fee:                 ₹1,125
Escrow interest (annual):   ₹4,500
Rent automation (annual):   ₹2,750
────────────────────────────────
TOTAL:                      ~₹8,500 per successful rental
```

**Compare to NoBroker**: They make ₹1,500 per user (subscription). We make **5-6x more** because we're transaction-linked.

## 7. What risks are we taking?

| Risk | How bad? | How we mitigate |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| **Users bypass the platform** | HIGH - They exchange numbers and deal offline | Anti-bypass technology blocks contact sharing; escrow makes on-platform deal better |
| **Not enough landlord supply** | HIGH - No properties = no tenants | Start hyper-local (1 neighborhood), door-to-door sales |
| **NoBroker copies us** | MEDIUM - They have ₹200M funding | They're subscription-focused; changing to escrow is hard pivot |
| **Escrow regulatory issues** | MEDIUM - RBI may require license | Partner with licensed escrow provider (Razorpay) |
| **High CAC, low retention** | MEDIUM - Expensive to acquire, use once | Rent automation creates monthly touchpoint; referrals lower CAC |
| **Deposit disputes** | LOW - Tenant/landlord disagree on deductions | Clear move-in photos, standardized deduction rules, arbitration |

### The Biggest Risk (and our biggest bet):
> **"Will Indian tenants and landlords trust a startup with ₹50,000-2,00,000?"**

Our answer: UPI normalized digital payments. People now trust Razorpay, Paytm, Google Pay with lakhs. We're riding that trust wave.

## 8. What metrics tell us if this is working?

### North Star Metric:
> **Monthly Successful Transactions (MST)** = Number of tenants who move into properties through Homie

### Leading Metrics (Early indicators):

| Metric | Target | What it tells us |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **Daily Active Users (DAU)** | 1,000+ | Are people using the app? |
| **Swipe Rate** | 15-25/session | Is the product engaging? |
| **Match Rate** | 15-20% | Is matching algorithm working? |
| **Message Response Rate** | 75%+ | Are landlords engaged? |
| **Viewing Conversion** | 50%+ | Are matches serious? |

### Lagging Metrics (Business health):

| Metric | Target | What it tells us |
|--------|--------|------------------|
| **GMV (Gross Merchandise Value)** | ₹50L+/month | Total deposits flowing through |
| **Revenue** | ₹2-3L/month | Are we monetizing? |
| **Take Rate** | 3-4% | Revenue as % of GMV |
| **CAC** | <₹1,000 | Cost to acquire customer |
| **LTV** | >₹6,000 | Lifetime value per customer |
| **LTV:CAC Ratio** | >6:1 | Unit economics healthy? |

### Red Flags (Stop and fix):
- Match rate <10% → Matching algorithm broken
- Viewing conversion <30% → Product-market fit issue
- Bypass rate >20% → Revenue leakage critical
- DAU declining week-over-week → Retention problem

### Success Milestone (for angel round):
```
✓ 100 successful transactions
✓ ₹50 lakh GMV through escrow
✓ 3-4% take rate proven
✓ 80% landlord list 2nd property (supply retention)
```
